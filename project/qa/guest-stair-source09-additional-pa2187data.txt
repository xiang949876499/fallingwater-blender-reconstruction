HABS No. PA-5346-A 
Location: State Route 381, Stewart Township, Ohiopyle vicinity, Fayette County, 
Pennsylvania 
Present Owner: 
and Occupant 
Western Pennsylvania Conservancy 
Present Use: Historic House Museum 
Significance: 
Historical Information and Architectural Information 
A. Physical History: 
1. Date of erection: 1939 
2. Architect: Frank Lloyd Wright 
3. Original owners: Edgar J. Kaufmann and Liliane S. Kaufmann 
Subsequent owners: Western Pennsylvania Conservancy 
B. Historical Context: 
F allingwater is one in a series of constructions that marks the resurgence of 
Wright's career.
