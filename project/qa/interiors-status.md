# Interior furnishing implementation

`scripts/furnishings.py` exports `build(ctx, rooms) -> list[bpy.types.Object]` and owns only `FW_FURN_*` objects and `40_FURNITURE_*` collections. Existing `fwlib` and `materials` are used without modification. Explicit furniture centers are world coordinates, angles are degrees, and local +Y is the back/headboard side.

The original furniture-only smoke test verified 4,372 objects across 60 room census records in Blender 5.2.1 LTS, including 4,115 meshes and 126 curves. All objects had room, asset, evidence and source tags, visible materials and finite transforms. `interiors-smoke-result.json` and `interiors-smoke.blend` preserve that diagnostic run; they are not the latest integrated scene or a finished presentation.

The subsequent pre-iteration04 main/guest/furnishing module check built 4,464 furnishing objects using corrected guest room records. `interiors-practical-check.json` records six actual ground/body-clearance passes at and approaching the corrected kitchen door: source x324/y270–287, center world y13.88565. AGA is south of the doorway at (−0.82,12.55), with the worktable and four farm chairs shifted north. These unmeasured placements remain C. The old misplaced doorway is closed by the architecture module. The final kitchen window-bay detail and the complete site/ceiling integration still require the new integrated-scene check.

The independent two-dimensional footprint checker initially found overlapping placements. Those were corrected, including role handling for bedroom terraces/closets, aligned theater chairs, the L-shaped guest bathroom, the narrow main north bathroom, and laundry equipment. The current proxy check reports zero floor furniture footprint intersections and zero polygon-fit warnings. The generated `interiors-plan-contact.png` was opened and visually inspected. This does not prove three-dimensional mesh clearance or navigable door/stair routes.

The module includes the main fixed benches and north dining niche, faceted emergent hearth rock and suspended red kettle, photograph-informed walnut casework/books/sling chair, St. Charles kitchen casework with real basin recesses, AGA/Frigidaire/KitchenAid appliance interpretations, four farm chairs, separate beds and casework, guest lounge screen and vertical wood lamp, sanitary fixtures, wine storage and readable service-room fittings. Empty circulation, foundation, water and ordinary terrace records deliberately receive no invented furniture.

All unmeasured furniture dimensions, exact appliance variants, small props, unseen backs, and unsupported service furnishings remain evidence C. Abstract framed fields are original placeholders, not copies of source artworks. `data/furnishing-evidence.json` contains the source and limitation register.

Eight dark service/closet spaces now receive replaceable C-level practical ceiling lights: main basement bath, boiler and wine, main coat closet, guest basement bath/laundry, guest upper bath/hall. Each has a visible porcelain plate/socket, transmissive clear bulb, small emissive filament and metal guard; a 9–32 W artistic point source sits within the visible bulb. Shape, placement, color and power are all C, with no claim to archival fixture identity. Materials `service_clear_bulb_C` and `service_filament_C` are separate from `lamp_diffuser`, so the lighting preset does not silently replace their emission settings. No hidden area-fill planes are introduced.

All eight fixtures passed five actual upward support/volume rays in the independent module scene; the point sources coincide with their visible bulbs. `interiors-practical-fixtures.json` records supporting object names, coordinates, power and provisional visual status. The lowest hardware is 1.842 m above the main basement floors and 1.932–2.212 m in the other tested rooms; these low ceilings and fixture proportions need rendered review. The diagnostic checkpoint is `interiors-practical-check.blend`, not the deliverable working scene.

Still required from integrated scene QA: inspect actual room renders, fixture/wall intersections, all door approach clearances, face orientation/lighting, and reference resemblance. No claim of photorealistic acceptance is made by this smoke/proxy pass.
